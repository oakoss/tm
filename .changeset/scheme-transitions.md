A Scheme file's `[workflows.ID]` can list the moves its Statuses allow, as `transitions = [{ from = "todo", to = ["in_progress", "cancelled"] }]`. A Workflow without the list, or a Status it does not name, allows any move, and `to = []` makes a Status final. `tm move` and the TUI refuse another move with `invalid_value`, naming the Statuses the Issue may move to; `tm move` refuses before reading piped text. `tm show`, the TUI's Status pickers and `tm scheme show` offer the same moves, and `tm scheme show --json` gives a Workflow's `transitions` as `"any"` or that list. `tm start`, a parent following its child, and `tm git scan` closing on merge take the first Status of the category the Workflow allows from where the Issue is: `tm start` is refused when there is none, `tm next` passes such an Issue over as `cannot_start` and `tm ready` leaves it out, a parent is left where it is with a warning, and `tm git scan` counts a merged Issue it cannot close as not moved and retries it on the next scan. `tm undo` and imports are not held to the transitions. An Issue in a Status its Workflow lacks may move to any Status, and `tm show` now lists them all. A Scheme file is refused at its line when a transition names a Status its Workflow lacks or lists a move twice.
